Definition of the broader concept “track routing”:
“Within the KBK component concept system, track routing serves as a KBK-specific organizational concept used to group the components and assemblies that direct, divert, transfer, stop, separate, lock, or otherwise control the movement of trolleys and loads within a track system. Track routing includes concepts such as track switch, turntable, drop section, stop station, and other movement-control concepts. Track routing differs from track sections because track routing controls movement through the track system rather than forming the travel path itself. Track routing differs from track connections because track routing directs or controls trolley movement rather than creating permanent structural connections between track sections.”
